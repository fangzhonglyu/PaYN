# BitMoD tile: area and power by function

Generated by `flow/bitmod_hier_report.py bm_20261016b` from `flow/bitmod_hier.sh bm_20261016b`; do not edit by hand.

The tile is the migrated BitMoD 8x8 tile (`designs/baselines/bitmod`, upstream bitmod @ 5cb051c) with each PE split into one submodule per function (`bitmod_pe_h.sv`, `pe_scratch_h`).  Every expression is copied verbatim; `tb/eq_pe_scratch_h.sv` checks every pipeline register and output of `pe_scratch_h` against the original `pe_scratch`, X-exact, every cycle, over 2 x 200,000 random cycles.  TSMC22 sc7mcpp140z SVT C30 + HPK.  The tile excludes the chip-level controller (Booth term generation, FP4 conversion, special values) and skew, as the BITMOD_TILE baseline does.

## Timing sign-off

The route was synthesized at 2.25 ns and fails there by a few ps (setup timing violation -0.018).  It is qualified at its 2.5 ns operating clock with ASTRAEA's rules unchanged: Innovus restores the final database, reproduces the route's own reports (setup -0.018 ns, hold +0.196 ns), and re-times only the clock (`flow/tcl/innovus_opclock_reports.tcl`).

| at 2.5 ns, uncertainty 0.125 ns | setup WNS (ns) | hold WNS (ns) |
|---|---:|---:|
| BitMoD tile, Innovus (gate) | +0.197 (latch-borrow checks 0) | +0.196 |
| BitMoD tile, PrimeTime (not gating) | +0.000 | -0.047 |
| FlexPosit core fp_20261015b, Innovus (gate) | +0.039 | +0.125 |
| FlexPosit core fp_20261015b, PrimeTime (not gating) | -0.120 | -0.048 |

PrimeTime reads the same netlist and SPEF with the synthesis SDC re-timed to 2.5 ns.  Its register-to-register slacks are lower than Innovus's by about the clock insertion delay: after CTS, CCOpt writes the I/O compensation as `set_clock_latency -source` (-0.27 ns here) for the -max condition only, and ASTRAEA times in OCV mode, where the setup capture clock and the hold launch clock take the unset -min value (0).  Innovus therefore shifts only one side of each register-to-register check, in the optimistic direction, on every ASTRAEA route alike, so the gate is applied to both designs the same way and PrimeTime is recorded beside it.  Both designs are within the 0.125 ns uncertainty in PrimeTime; the gate-level runs below use the routed SDF (real clock-tree delays, no uncertainty) and must be free of timing-check violations (gl-audit).

## Caveats

* The split adds module boundaries, which limit cross-boundary optimization; the flat-vs-split synthesis totals above measure that cost.  Shares are the point of this report, not absolute totals.
* The power route was synthesized and routed at a tighter clock than 2.5 ns (SYN_PERIOD in the campaign log) because the earlier BITMOD_TILE routes missed 2.5 ns and their gate-level runs went X on stage-1 setup violations.  It is simulated and measured at 400 MHz; the over-constraint upsizes cells, so its absolute area and power are somewhat above a design closed exactly at 2.5 ns.
* Tile only: the chip-level controller and skew network (1.3% of the 4x4-tile array's area in the collaborator's data) are outside this DUT.
* Uniform random operands, like every bench in this repo; real LLM weights and activations toggle less.
* The power bench relaunches the controller's operand data 2-state (X to 0): the controller's resetless term registers otherwise held X on ~9 idle beats inside the window (the tile ignores non-valid beats; X toggles are not activity).  X operands on a valid beat are fatal.  Control signals are unchanged.
* Routed GL approval: one -2 ps extracted INTERCONNECT value on a drain-row output-port wire, clamped to 0 (`--approve-negative-iopath-clamp-ps=2`; `build/flow/bitmod_hier/<campaign>/gl_validator_args_rationale.txt`).
